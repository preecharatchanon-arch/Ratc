CSV format and units

Required columns: name, lat, lon. Coordinates use decimal degrees. Optional: demand, role, address, service_minutes, earliest, latest. Use role=depot for one depot row, or keep the current depot.

Times may use 08:00 and 17:00. For seconds, use service_s, earliest_s, latest_s. Demand defaults to 0; demand and capacity must use the same units. Invalid rows are rejected without replacing your current plan.
