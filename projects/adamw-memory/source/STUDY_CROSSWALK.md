## Scope exclusions

- NEXUS I and NEXUS II Stages 01–05 investigated different representation and macrostate hypotheses. They are not cited as evidence for the manuscript's low-rank AdamW-control conclusions and are deliberately excluded to avoid presenting exploratory studies as confirmatory support.
- Stage 08 was a partial branch and Stage 09 was a planned branch; neither supplies a completed result used in the paper.
- Large model checkpoints, optimizer tensors, dataset images and duplicate full archives are excluded to meet the OpenReview size limit. The retained arrays are the smallest primary payloads needed to recalculate the reported response geometry without treating derived CSV tables as raw evidence.
- The Stage 14 claim is confined to its reported eight-history stable CIFAR10 cohort. No stable-CIFAR100 observation is asserted.

## Independence and interpretation

Training histories/seeds are the independent units. Future paths and intervention signs are paired measurements within a history, not additional independent samples. Stage 06's potency statistic is not an accuracy improvement and is not low-rank projection evidence. Its scalar-prediction comparator result is a separate analysis. Stages 07 and 10–14 provide the manuscript's direct low-rank control evidence.
