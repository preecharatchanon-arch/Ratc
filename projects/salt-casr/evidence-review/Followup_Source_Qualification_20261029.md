# Follow up on functional and human sensory evidence

29 October 2026. Four additional primary cases were assessed against the
measurement requirements for the CaSR-to-human-saltiness comparison.
One full article and its original supplement were recovered. No new scientific
numerical test was eligible or executed; the previous functional contrast was
not rerun. Evidence acquisition changed; the matched-selector gate did not.

## New human panel with a different receptor measurement

Cao et al., DOI10.1016/j.fochx.2026.104238, PMCIDPMC13445481: full XML, SI,
Figure4A/B and SI TablesS6/S7 inspected, including rendered SI pages23/24.
Twelve trained assessors evaluated a16-peptide screen; triplicate evaluations
do not create36 people. Peptide1mg/mL in nominal0.3%NaCl,pH6.5 was the
saltiness condition. NaCl percentage basis and total sodium remain unresolved.
VEK,VVVD,GE,LE are author-reported saltiness-enhancing examples, not four
independently confirmed hits or labels for all remaining peptides. Human
Figure4B has bubble values without paired uncertainty or a NaCl-only row;
S6/S7 numerical saltiness is electronic-tongue output. Dose-response figures
concern umami. Cellular work concerns TAS1R1, with CaSR only a modeling template.
No measured CaSR potency is added. Raw data are offered on request. Peptide-only
profiling at10mg/mL cannot isolate intrinsic contributions at the1mg/mL mixture
dose. This is a sensory acquisition lead, not a qualified CaSR-selection panel.

The molecular identities must remain exact. Conventional LE and GE sequences
must not be joined to gamma-Glu-Leu or gamma-Glu-Gly. Sequence reversal and
gamma versus alpha linkage are material differences. Targeted lookup of the
four author-highlighted sequences found no eligible matched functional hCaSR
record in our registry or this bounded primary search. That is not proof that
such measurements do not exist. Do not record zero activity.

## Two new sources remain acquisition leads

Yang et al., DOI10.1016/j.lwt.2026.119987, October2026, advertises four synthesized
chicken-soup peptides, sensory work, CD and MD. Reported threshold ranges refer
to kokumi across matrices; they cannot be assigned to individual saltiness
outcomes. Its abstract does not establish measured CaSR function. Full methods
and usable numerical data did not recover. Crossref confirms an OA license,
but the advertised PDF and XML links returned195-byte Site Unavailable HTML.
The indexed data statement refers to a link at submission without exposing a
resolved dataset URL here. Keep all unverified fields missing, not negative.

Xu et al., DOI10.1016/j.foodres.2026.119732, October2026, distinguishes intrinsic
saltiness, enhancement and interaction in sausage-derived peptides. The indexed
primary text concerns ENaC/TMC4 modeling; it does not establish our paired CaSR
measurement requirement. Exact sequences behind short codes, human numerical
comparisons, uncertainty and interaction calculations remain unqualified. No
repository manuscript was listed by OpenAlex. The advertised publisher XML
returned the same195-byte error page. No synergy result or dose grid was
reconstructed from abstract claims.

## A functional peptide patent in another species

US20220256889A1, Examples1–5 and Tables2–4, reports feline CaSR assays, including
phosphorylated peptides. Calcium-free HEK-derived reporter assays used at least
two occasions and four technical repetitions per concentration. Fitted EC50
standard errors are not automatically biological SEMs. Some values are only
approximate. Feeding outcomes belong to casein hydrolysates in animal matrices,
not individual human peptide saltiness. Broad human-receptor wording in patent
claims is not a human assay result. Asp-Val-Glu values differ between source
Table2 and Table4; keep fractions/synthetic assays separate. A source-listed
inactive fraction lacks sufficient assay bounds for a human negative label.
Inventors overlap the Laffitte source network; independence is not presumed.
This supplies contextual feline functional evidence, not matched human labels.

## Decisions for the next calculation

The important distinction is between a useful sensory lead, an eligible
measurement and a validated selector. A source can contain real human taste
work and still fail the proposed CaSR-to-saltiness test. A full supplement with
precise instrument numbers does not repair missing human responses. Likewise,
a real CaSR assay in another species does not fill a human receptor field.

For the16-peptide panel, the next acquisition item is a numerical HUMAN table
for the0.3%NaCl comparator and each peptide mixture, with assessor/session
structure, paired differences or covariance and the associated dose/sodium
metadata. It becomes relevant to the CaSR-selector test only if exact compound
identities also have measured, interpretable hCaSR activity under specified
conditions. The current evidence does not establish those paired records.
Recovering just one of these halves would still not unlock the full comparison.

For the previous N102F test, the published availability statement points to
structural deposits and the recovered SI contains summaries, not unrounded
experiment-level functional values. No new qualified raw-response source was
located. The earlier conditional small-n calculation remains unchanged.

Do not pursue a new numerical test on these sources now. Apply the existing
`Source_Assessment_and_Design.md` and `Sensory_Window_Assessment_and_Design.md`
only after eligibility passes. A source-independent test must compare selection
with versus without measured CaSR information using the same eligible candidates
and sensory endpoint. Practical useful-gain margins must be justified for the
chosen application before execution; no margin is manufactured from this search.
Chemistry, dose, matrix and source overlap are competing explanations. Family/
source dependence and within-person uncertainty remain distinct units.

If an eligible panel and practical margin become available, freeze the exact
comparison before fitting. A useful incremental gain would justify independent
confirmation in the defined application. An interval excluding useful gain
would close that feature/domain; a broad interval would remain inconclusive.
Model selection on the same inspected material would require a separate exploratory analysis and independent confirmation.
Until the prerequisite changes, keep numerical testing paused rather than run
another docking screen, proxy-label predictor or electronic-tongue-to-human fit.
