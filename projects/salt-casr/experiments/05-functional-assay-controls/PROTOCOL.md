# Analysis scope — functional assay controls

Retrospective evidence reanalysis, 15 October 2026. The primary sources and their reported outcomes were inspected before writing this scope; this is not a preregistration or an independent replication.

Questions:

1. Which explicitly tested peptide-dose/NaCl combinations increased the fraction of responding cultured human taste cells over the authors’ SUM comparator?
2. Does a fall in the raw mixture response after knockdown establish that the peptide-specific contribution disappeared?
3. Do reported sensory enhancement and the tested organoid endpoint agree for the same three soy sequences, and what assay information is still missing?

Use six attributed input tables in `data/functional/`. Categorical non-significance is distinct from numerical zero. Untested doses and inaccessible methods remain missing. Main-text categorical statements can be expanded only over the explicitly stated tested condition combinations. Preserve peptide-alone cellular EC50 separately from enhancement and binding affinity.

Calculate counts and descriptive percentage-point contrasts. Keep Figure 7a-c and 7d-f cohorts separate; do not substitute one cohort’s baseline into the other’s mixture. Retain source significance calls without deriving new p-values from pooled cell counts. Do not calculate a cross-study accuracy score, estimate sodium replacement, fit a new model or claim a receptor-binding pose.

The output is a justified choice of comparators and assay conditions, with a reproducible calculation and identified missing evidence. No new peptide activity or safety is measured here.
