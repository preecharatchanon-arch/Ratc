(20 discovery tasks, 30 untouched confirmation tasks). Earlier experiments are design
history and are not pooled into the final estimator.

Why the full historical ZIP archives are not duplicated here:
  They are substantially less reviewer-friendly, contain many redundant per-task artifacts,
  and are not required to reproduce the main-paper inference. Their canonical identities are
  preserved in evidence/06_evidence_lineage.json. This supplement favors concise, inspectable
  evidence rather than an archive dump.
